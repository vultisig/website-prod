# Welcome to Vultisig Articles

We're excited to introduce our new articles system! This allows us to share insights, updates, and deep dives directly on the Vultisig website, improving SEO and providing a better experience for our community.

## What You Can Do

With this new system, you can:

- **Create articles** using our admin interface
- **Share insights** about MPC wallets, security, and blockchain technology
- **Improve SEO** by hosting content directly on our domain
- **Engage with the community** through well-formatted, accessible content

## Features

The articles system includes:

1. **Markdown Support** - Write articles using Markdown for easy formatting
2. **Image Support** - Add featured images to make your articles stand out
3. **Tagging System** - Organize articles with tags
4. **SEO Optimized** - Each article has proper metadata for search engines
5. **Responsive Design** - Articles look great on all devices

## Getting Started

To create your first article, visit the [admin page](/articles/admin) and start writing!
